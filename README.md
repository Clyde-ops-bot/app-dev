# My Favorite Movies

---

## 1. The Karate Kid
**The Karate Kid** is a martial arts or drama film. It shows how *bravery*, *strength*, *perseverance* under pressure and proper guidance can help anyone overcome bullying.

> "Everything is Kung Fu" - *Mr. Jackie Chan*

---

## 2. 3 Idiots
**3 Idiots** is an inspirational comedy-drama about three engineering students. It highlights the importance of bond or friendship, as well as pursuing true passion instead of just racing for grades.

> "Pursue excellence, and success will follow." - *Rancho*

### Important Lessons:
* Follow your passion.
* True friendship matters.
* `All Izz Well!`

---

## 3. Home Alone
**Home Alone** is a Christmas comedy movie about *Kevin McCallister*, a young boy who accidentally gets left behind during Christmas vacation and defends his home from Thiefs.

1. Good Strategies and Funny traps.
2. Great holiday movie for the family.
